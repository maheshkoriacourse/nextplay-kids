Our story

Started on a Mumbai ground,
built for every child

NextPlay began in 2021 when two parents searched for a size-safe cricket helmet for their 8-year-olds and found adult marketing wearing kid sizes. We decided gear, programs and coaching should be built around children — their growth, their courage, their joy — with parents holding the pen.

🌅

What we believe

A child who feels safe tries things. A child who is encouraged keeps trying. A child who belongs to a team learns the biggest lesson sport can teach — that showing up for each other matters more than any scoreline.

Where we play

Mumbai-first, India-ready

From Andheri turf to Powai pool to Shivaji Park nets, our programs run where families actually are — monsoon-proof and society-friendly. And our gear ships to every pin code in India.

Our values

Six promises we keep

🛡

Safety first

Certified gear, fit guides, two-adult sessions, consent everything. Safety is the foundation, not a feature.

💪

Confidence

We praise effort, courage and teamwork — never appearance, never weight, never a fear of losing.

😄

Joy

If the session ends with a child asking “again tomorrow?”, we did our job. Fun is the retention strategy.

♿

Inclusion

Adaptive sessions, seated formats, sensory-friendly spaces. Every child deserves a place to play — every child, literally.

📈

Skill development

Age-grade curricula written with certified coaches. Progress measured against the child’s own last week, not other kids.

🤝

Community

Parent circles, coach mentorship and school partnerships — the whole village that raises a player.

Inclusive & adaptive sport

Every body plays

Sunday adaptive sessions with 1:2 coaching ratios, seated volleyball, sound-based balls for visually impaired players, sensory-friendly hours and therapist-on-call support. If your child needs an adjustment, that’s not an exception — that’s our Tuesday-morning planning meeting.
