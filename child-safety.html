Our promise to families

Child-safety policy

Last updated: September 2026. Child safety is not a page on our website — it is the operating system of every session we run.

🛡 Report anything, anytime: safeguarding@nextplaykids.example or our 24×7 helpline. Every report is acknowledged within 24 hours and investigated by a designated safeguarding officer — never by the person involved.

1. Who we entrust with your children

✅ Every coach completes police background verification, safeguarding certification and annual first-aid training before their first session. ✅ Coach–child ratios never exceed 1:8 (1:4 for swimming, 1:2 for adaptive sessions). ✅ Two-adult rule: no single adult alone with a child, ever — including travel to events. ✅ Coach certification records are shown to parents on request. ✅ Annual re-verification and refresher training for all program staff.

2. Communication boundaries

💬 Coaches message parents only, through approved in-app channels that are logged and visible to you. 🚫 No personal phone numbers, no private social-media contact, no closed groups with children. 📸 Photo/video consent is opt-in per child, purpose-limited, and revocable anytime from the parent dashboard. 🚫 No one-to-one digital communication between any adult and any child on our platform — ever.

3. Physical-safety standards

🪖 Certified helmets and protective gear for every activity that needs them — no exceptions, including trials. 🥊 Contact/sparring activities only with explicit parental consent, certified supervision and full gear. 🧯 First-aid kit + certified first-aider at every session and event; emergency contacts accessible to venue lead. 🌡 Heat, rain and air-quality protocols: hydration breaks, shade rotation, indoor backup plans. ♿ Accessibility adjustments planned per child in advance — not improvised on the day.

4. Emotional safety

🗣 Effort-based praise; zero tolerance for body-shaming, weight-based comments or humiliation from anyone — coach, child or parent. 🏆 Winning is celebrated; winning is never required. Participation and growth matter more than trophies. 🧩 Sensory-friendly options at every camp: quiet zones, ear defenders on request, no forced group performance. 🤝 Anti-bullying module built into martial arts and team programs — kids learn to stand up for each other.

5. How to raise a concern

1️⃣ Email safeguarding@nextplaykids.example or call the helpline — both monitored 24×7. 2️⃣ Acknowledged within 12 hours; a safeguarding officer (independent of the program) takes the case. 3️⃣ The concerned coach is stood down from the affected sessions while we investigate — no exceptions. 4️⃣ Findings shared with the reporting family; statutory authorities engaged where law requires. 5️⃣ Outcomes recorded in our audit log; policy updated when we learn better.

6. Wellness guidance, never medical advice

Our coaches and physiotherapist partners give general wellness guidance (fit, warm-ups, hydration, recovery habits). This is not medical advice. Injuries, pain, developmental concerns or anything medical belongs with your paediatrician — and any child reporting pain sits out immediately, no questions asked.

7. This prototype’s data promise

This website is a front-end prototype: carts, quiz answers and dashboard samples live only in your browser’s local storage and are never transmitted anywhere. No real payments are processed. See the privacy policy for how the live service will handle family data.
