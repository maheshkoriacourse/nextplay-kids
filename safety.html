Safety-first promise

Safety & sizing, measured not guessed

Co-written with paediatric physiotherapists. Measure with a soft tape, keep a thumb’s width of growing room, and re-check every season — children grow in bursts.

⚠ Wellness, not medicine: This guide helps parents fit gear and prevent common play injuries. It is general wellness guidance from coaches and physiotherapists — it is not medical advice. For pain, head injuries, breathing trouble or anything that worries you, see a qualified paediatric doctor. When in doubt, sit the session out.
The two-finger rule

🪖 Helmet fit in 5 steps

Measure the head

Soft tape around the forehead, just above the eyebrows and ears — the widest point.

Two fingers above brows

The front rim sits two finger-widths above the eyebrows, level — never tilted back.

No wobble

Side-to-side shake test: skin moves, helmet doesn’t. Buckle first, then shake.

Strap check

Y-buckle sits just below each ear. Chin strap snug: one finger, no more, slides under.

Re-fit each season

Growth spurts change fit fast. Re-check every 3–4 months and after any crash — replace after impacts.
