Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Applies to nextplaykids.in and all NextPlay programs. Questions? Contact our data officer.

🛡 The one-line version: Parents are the account holders. We collect the minimum data needed to run great programs, we never sell family data, children never receive direct messages, and you can export or delete everything anytime.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers parents and guardians (our account holders), their children who participate in NextPlay programs or use NextPlay gear, and visitors to our website. We operate in India and follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and COPPA-aligned practices for any cross-border users.

2. What we collect (and why)

DataWhy we need itWho sees it
Parent name, contact, delivery addressOrders, program booking, order updatesOnly NextPlay staff fulfilling your request

We do not collect: caste, religion, precise geolocation of children, behavioural advertising profiles of children, or anything we don’t actively need.

3. Parental consent is the engine

✅ Accounts: only parents/guardians 18+ can create accounts and place orders. Children participate; parents decide. ✅ Photography: opt-in per child, purpose-limited (progress records), revocable anytime from the parent dashboard. ✅ Coach messaging: coaches message parents, never children. All threads are logged and reviewable by you. ✅ Data-driven recommendations: off by default. Turn it on and we use training data to suggest programs; off means generic suggestions. ✅ Withdrawal: withdraw any consent from the dashboard or by email — programs affected stop that feature immediately, no penalty.

4. Children’s rights (yes, they have some)

As children grow, they gain their own data dignity. When a child on our platform reaches an age of digital consent (14 in India under DPDP), we will begin asking them as well as you for consent on new data uses. Children can always ask us to delete their achievements record — and we’ll loop you in, together.

5. Sharing (spoiler: barely any)

🚫 We never sell family data. Not for coupons, not for “partners”, not ever. 📦 Delivery partners see only name + address + phone, for the delivery. 🏫 School partners see roster-level data only for their own enrolled students. ⚖️ Legal: only if law compels us — and we’ll tell you unless gagged by law.

6. Your controls

📥 Export: one tap in the parent dashboard — machine-readable copy of everything we hold. 🗑 Delete: account deletion removes personal data within 30 days; safety/financial records kept only as law requires. 🔧 Correct: edit any profile field anytime; coach rosters update instantly. 📵 Unsubscribe: one tap in any email. Marketing off ≠ service messages off (we still confirm orders).

7. Security & retention

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is role-limited (a football coach sees football rosters, not the whole school), and we run quarterly safeguarding audits. We keep child profiles only while enrolled or 24 months after the last session, whichever is later — then it’s gone.
